## SEARCH-stage synthesis: what learning is actually adding
[AI判断] The retained literature suggests that the strongest pursuit-specific case for learning is **not** “neural networks outperform classical pursuit laws” in the abstract. Learning is repeatedly introduced where classical/game/control formulations become brittle or expensive under one or more of the following:

- **partial/local observations and occlusion** where a closed-form feedback law does not receive the full game state;
- **coordination dimension explosion** where joint state/action/game PDEs or combinatorial assignments scale poorly;
- **unknown/adaptive opponent behavior** where a fixed evader model is an unrealistic assumption;
- **coupled discrete–continuous decisions** such as target allocation / role choice / maneuver control;
- **high-dimensional physical dynamics** where kinematic pursuit geometry alone is insufficient for executable UAV control;
- **sparse success signals / hard exploration** in fast-evader, obstacle-rich, 3D tasks, motivating curriculum, imitation, self-supervision and model-based rollouts;
- **communication constraints / information asymmetry** motivating learned messages, graph aggregation, mean-field approximations, and mutual advising;
- **generalization across team size / environments / evader behaviors**, where fixed handcrafted strategies require re-tuning;
- **safety constraints**, where CBF/game structure can shield or shape RL rather than be discarded;
- **sim-to-real dynamics mismatch**, where hardware-aligned simulators, direct low-level command interfaces and domain randomization become part of the learning system.

[AI判断] Conversely, papers that merely swap MADDPG→MAPPO→SAC or add attention without changing any of these task assumptions contribute much less to the literature map.
