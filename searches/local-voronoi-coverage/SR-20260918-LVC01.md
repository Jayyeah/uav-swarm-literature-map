#### A2. Pratissoli, Capelli, Sabattini — On Coverage Control for Limited Range Multi-Robot Systems
- Venue/year: IEEE/RSJ IROS 2022, pp. 9957–9963.
- DOI: 10.1109/IROS47612.2022.9982002.
- Why serious: closest direct match to CoCap B2 geometry.

- Venue/year: IEEE/RSJ IROS 2012, pp. 3546–3552.
- DOI: 10.1109/IROS.2012.6385850.
- Why serious: directly computes a range-constrained Voronoi cell from local robots.

- Venue/year: Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems 64:603–623, 2011.
- DOI: 10.1007/s10846-010-9537-1.

- [原文/Web核验] For identifying the full current Voronoi cell from an initially unknown network, the decentralized range-adjustment procedure stops when the communication range reaches twice the distance from p_i to the farthest currently computed Voronoi vertex (or the sensed region covers the domain); the authors argue farther newly discovered agents then cannot alter the cell.

# LOCAL VORONOI TAXONOMY

### V0 — Global Voronoi
V_i(P) = {q in Q : ||q-p_i|| <= ||q-p_j|| for all j}. Full set of sites (or an equivalent exact distributed reconstruction) determines the whole cell. Standard centroidal Voronoi/Lloyd coverage lives here.

### V1 — Neighbor-only exact Voronoi
The whole ordinary cell can be reconstructed from the true Voronoi/Delaunay neighbors because non-neighbors contribute redundant half-planes. Important operational caveat: knowing that only true neighbors matter is not the same as knowing which agents are those neighbors under an a priori fixed finite range. Exact distributed algorithms or adaptive range expansion address the discovery problem.

### V2 — Radius-limited exact/local Voronoi
Given current neighbor positions for all j with ||p_j-p_i|| <= Rc, ownership inside a protected local disk is exact when Rv <= Rc/2 in the Euclidean setting. Agents farther than Rc cannot place their perpendicular bisector inside B(p_i,Rv), because any q in that ball has ||q-p_i|| <= Rv while triangle inequality gives ||q-p_j|| >= ||p_j-p_i||-Rv > Rc-Rv >= Rv.

This is local-region exactness, not full-cell exactness.

### V3 — Truncated Voronoi
The most literature-aligned CoCap definition is

V_i^loc = Q ∩ B(p_i,Rv) ∩ ⋂_{j: ||p_j-p_i||<=Rc} {q : ||q-p_i|| <= ||q-p_j||}.

Under Euclidean distance, exact current positions, and Rc >= 2Rv, this equals

Q ∩ B(p_i,Rv) ∩ V_i(P).

Pratissoli 2022 uses the same structure with sensing/information radius R and truncated radius r=R/2; Guruprasad 2012 independently uses other robots within twice the sensor-circle radius.

### V4 — Approximate/local heuristic
Construct a nominal “Voronoi cell” only from peers inside Rc but do not clip evaluation to Rv <= Rc/2. Outside the protected disk, an unseen farther peer can alter ownership, so full-cell/centroid exactness is not guaranteed. This class should not be called exact local Voronoi without a certificate.

### V5 — Obstacle/geodesic local Voronoi
Ownership is defined in free space using geodesic/visibility-constrained distance or another obstacle-aware construction. Existing non-convex coverage work shows this is a real separate branch. The Euclidean Rc >= 2Rv proof does not automatically transfer: Euclidean neighbor distance, geodesic influence, visibility and disconnected free-space components can diverge.

# RADIUS CONDITION AUDIT

## Canonical distinction
- Rs — target/obstacle sensing radius: what non-friendly physical entities can be observed.
- Rc — friendly communication / friend-position acquisition radius: which teammate states are available to i.
- Rv — Voronoi computation/truncation radius: spatial region around p_i in which ownership is evaluated and centroid/integrals are formed.
- Ru / interaction radius — radius over which the controller directly couples motions/connectivity constraints. This can equal Rc in a particular paper, but should not be conflated by definition.

Many papers overload “sensing radius” to mean the radius at which neighboring robot positions become available. CoCap should preserve these four symbols even when an implementation sets two equal.

## Rc >= 2Rv EVIDENCE STATUS
Status: DIRECT LITERATURE SUPPORT FOUND for the Euclidean local-cell exactness statement; not a newly invented CoCap theorem.

Evidence chain:
1. Cortés–Martínez–Bullo 2005 defines r-limited Delaunay interactions with Voronoi cells clipped by B(p_i,r/2) and establishes spatial distribution over the r-disk graph. Geometric reasoning in that line says agents farther than r cannot affect ownership within the r/2 disk.
2. Guruprasad–Dasgupta 2012 explicitly constructs the range-constrained cell of sensor radius r from robots within distance 2r.
3. Pratissoli–Capelli–Sabattini 2022 sets local information/sensing range R and truncated radius r=R/2; Lemma 1 states the computed limited cell equals the exact global Voronoi cell intersected with B(p_i,r).

Therefore CoCap notation maps naturally to Rc=R and Rv=r, giving Rc >= 2Rv as an easy sufficient condition. Equality is the canonical tight geometric protection radius for this argument; larger Rc is also sufficient.

### What it guarantees
It guarantees exact ordinary Euclidean Voronoi ownership inside B(p_i,Rv) when all friendly positions inside Rc are available and current. It does NOT by itself guarantee the entire V_i(P), the global cell centroid, communication-graph connectivity, or performance of a learned controller.

### Space/domain conditions
The pure half-plane distance argument is Euclidean. The coverage theorems in Pratissoli 2022 explicitly formulate Q as a convex polygonal planar environment; the Cortés 2005 coverage formulation is also in standard Euclidean coverage geometry. Bounded Q is handled by intersection with Q, provided its relevant boundary is known/sensed for the centroid/integral. Obstacles/geodesic distance require a separate proof.

### Boundary knowledge
For ownership against other agents, no global boundary is needed. To compute a centroid of Q ∩ B(p_i,Rv) ∩ V_i, the portion of Q/boundary intersecting the local disk must be known or locally sensed. Pratissoli motivates an unknown environment precisely by local environment sensing.

### Synchrony and mobility
The static geometric equality is pointwise-in-time. It assumes neighbor positions correspond to the same configuration. Packet delay, stale coordinates, asynchronous samples and fast mobile neighbors introduce state-estimation error; none of the located 2× statements directly certifies delayed communication. A robust margin Rc > 2Rv + motion/error allowance would be an engineering extension, not a verified theorem from this search.

### Necessary vs sufficient
2× is sufficient for local ownership exactness and geometrically sharp for a worst-case unseen point agent: a competitor just outside 2Rv can have its bisector just outside Rv, while one at/below 2Rv may touch/influence the protected disk. It is not necessary for a particular configuration because actual distant agents may be shielded by nearer ones.

### Entire-cell exactness is a different radius condition
Stergiopoulos–Tzes 2011 and the Adjust-Sensing-Radius lineage treat discovery of all true Delaunay neighbors/full cell. Their range is configuration-dependent; one stopping certificate is twice the distance from p_i to the farthest current cell vertex. That should not be rewritten as a fixed Rc=2Rv law unless Rv is literally that cell-extent certificate.

# NO-NEIGHBOR FALLBACK EVIDENCE

Direct coverage literature found no strong theorem for “last valid centroid memory,” “frontier anchor,” or “random exploration” as a canonical fallback.

The strongest direct geometric evidence is implicit in the limited-cell definition:
- If N_i within Rc is empty, the V3 half-plane intersection has no teammate constraints, so V_i^loc = Q ∩ B(p_i,Rv).
- Under locally uniform density and when the entire disk lies in the interior of Q, its centroid is exactly p_i.
- [AI判断] Therefore pure move-to-truncated-centroid gives u_i=0 in that special isolated/interior/uniform case. This is not merely a possible implementation bug; it is the natural symmetry equilibrium of the truncated objective. Calling “no neighbor -> centroid=self” a robust exploration fallback is therefore unsafe: it can freeze.
- Boundary clipping or non-uniform density breaks the symmetry and can generate a nonzero centroid vector.

Adjacent, non-coverage evidence: Bertrand et al. 2020 explicitly switches modes at N_i=0, skips Voronoi computation and tracks an exogenous waypoint; N_i=1 uses virtual robots. This supports the general engineering pattern “isolation must have an explicit fallback/reference,” but not any specific CoCap coverage fallback.

Current evidence status:
- last-valid centroid: no direct support found in this focused search; engineering heuristic.
- map centroid: theoretically corresponds to single-agent global CVT only if the agent is truly the only site / has global domain knowledge; invalid as a statement about temporarily disconnected communication.
- frontier/exploration anchor: plausible for unknown-space exploration, but no direct Tier-A support found here.
- self-centered local disk centroid: directly implied by limited Voronoi definitions; known freeze-by-symmetry risk under uniform interior conditions.
- reconnect behavior: connectivity-preserving papers avoid disconnection rather than proving a recovery rule after arbitrary disconnection.

# SCALE / DENSITY FINDINGS

1. Pratissoli 2022 provides a qualitative but relevant density statement: for fixed local radius r, sufficiently many agents such that the union of B(p_i,r) covers Q makes the limited and exact Voronoi partitions coincide; experiments show higher N reduces the gap to standard global coverage. This is an existence/coverage condition, not a closed-form N-to-radius scaling law.

2. A natural geometric spacing scale in a 2-D domain is l = sqrt(A/N). Therefore Rc / sqrt(A/N) is a sensible dimensionless engineering parameter for comparing 4v1, 8v2, 12v3 across area/density changes. [AI判断] This normalization is dimensional/geometric; the focused coverage papers found do not establish a universal theorem saying Rc must equal a fixed constant times sqrt(A/N).

3. Random-geometric-graph theory gives a stricter reference for global connectivity under iid random placements. Recent Penrose/Yang/Higgs results state n M_n^d / log n tends to a geometry-dependent constant; in d=2 this means connectivity radius is order sqrt(A log N / N), not merely sqrt(A/N). This is a stochastic connectivity threshold, not a Voronoi-coverage theorem and not directly representative of already-spread centroidal configurations.

4. Consequences for CoCap scaling:
- fixed area, N increasing: fixed Rc corresponds to increasing Rc/sqrt(A/N), hence more local neighbors on average and easier connectivity/local exactness.
- fixed density, area proportional to N: sqrt(A/N) stays constant. Fixed Rc preserves an O(1) local length scale/expected degree, but iid global connectivity over an ever-larger swarm is not automatically preserved; random-geometric connectivity adds a slowly growing sqrt(log N) factor relative to mean spacing.
- deterministic/lattice-like CVT deployments can maintain local adjacency with Rc = O(sqrt(A/N)); do not import random iid connectivity thresholds as a necessary condition for all controlled formations.

# COCAP B2 METHOD IMPLICATIONS

## A. Does Final IQN coverage centroid depend on the global friendly set?
Yes, in the task-relevant 9c8f603 branch.

[Repo evidence] src/cocap_voradj/envs/voronoi_adjacency.py::_voronoi_map builds the ownership of every grid point by argmin distance over all included sites. Under VCT-LS, _coverage_voronoi_map excludes evaders as sites but still includes all active pursuers. _pack_agent_obs then reads the resulting coverage centroid.

Thus Final VCT-LS is “pursuer-only global-friendly Voronoi” for the coverage centroid, while enemy/obstacle perception is local. It is not yet radius-limited friendly Voronoi.

## B. If only friends with ||p_j-p_i|| <= Rc remain, what is still exact?
- Exact: ownership, adjacency boundaries, centroid/integrals restricted to Q ∩ B(p_i,Rv), if Rv <= Rc/2 and all positions inside Rc are current.
- Not generally exact: the whole V_i(P), its global centroid, area, or ordinary full-cell Delaunay neighbor set.
- Full-cell exactness can be recovered only when all actual influencing Voronoi neighbors are known or a configuration-dependent range certificate has been met.

## C. Is B(p_i,Rv) ∩ V_i enough to produce a stable coverage gradient?
There is direct classical theory that centroid motion on an exact truncated/local cell is a valid gradient-like controller for a modified limited-range coverage objective and converges/monotonically improves that objective (Cortés 2005; Pratissoli 2022).

Boundary: this does not transfer a theorem to CoCap’s IQN simply because the centroid feature is supplied to a learned policy. It establishes that the feature itself corresponds to a coherent local coverage objective. Small Rv can intentionally optimize a different, more local objective and can be globally suboptimal.

## D. Which no-neighbor fallback has the strongest evidence?
No universal winner was found. The theorem-aligned V3 behavior is local disk/boundary centroid, but it provably/symmetrically freezes in the uniform interior isolated case. The best adjacent explicit implementation evidence is an external-reference mode switch. Last-valid centroid/frontier memory remains an engineering design, not a literature theorem identified here.

## E. Evidence boundary for B2 minimum implementation
SEARCH does not make the engineering decision. If MASTER wants the smallest mechanism with the cleanest theorem lineage, V3 exact truncated cell is substantially better supported than an unclipped “Voronoi over whatever friends happen to be inside Rc.” A memory/frontier isolation fallback would be a separate policy choice and should be labeled heuristic unless SCREEN finds a direct coverage source.

# OBSTACLE / BOUNDED ENVIRONMENT FINDINGS

- Bounded convex Q is the cleanest setting for the limited-cell theorem line.
- Breitenmoser 2010 establishes that non-convex polygonal environments require additional path/geometric handling; Lloyd + TangentBug can recover convergence to a local optimum and was experimentally demonstrated.
- Bhattacharya–Michael–Kumar 2013 uses generalized Voronoi in unknown non-convex environments with a distributed discrete representation.
- Geodesic Voronoi work in non-convex/mixed-dimensional domains further confirms that ordinary Euclidean Voronoi is not the only meaningful partition once obstacles alter reachable distance.
- Pratissoli 2022 itself explicitly says its non-convex test is preliminary and further study is needed.
- Therefore do not cite Rc >= 2Rv as an obstacle/geodesic theorem. At most it remains a Euclidean line-of-sight/local-position sufficient condition in free subregions; free-space metric exactness needs its own derivation.

# CONFLICTS / UNCERTAINTIES
- Terminology conflict: “sensing radius” in coverage papers often means sensing other robot positions, whereas CoCap Rs is reserved for enemy/obstacle sensing. Translate literature R to CoCap Rc only when the sensed quantity is friendly position.
- “Limited Voronoi” is not standardized. Some papers mean V_i ∩ B(p_i,r); others mean a sensor-range constrained ownership cell; others mean an approximate local computation. Definitions must be copied exactly during SCREEN.
- “Optimal” in limited-range papers can mean optimum/local maximum of a modified truncated objective, not equality to the global Lloyd optimum.
- Connectivity-preserving coverage can intentionally converge to suboptimal coverage due to connectivity constraints.
- The 2× local-cell result assumes exact current geometry. Communication delay/motion error is outside the verified claim.
- ResearchGate was used for discovery/full-text snippets on a few older papers; SCREEN should prefer publisher/author PDFs for theorem verification.
