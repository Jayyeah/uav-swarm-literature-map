## TAXONOMY CHANGES
1. **Recommend promoting B7 from provisional to stable.** Independent, persistent subliteratures exist for distance/range-only, bearing-only, GPS-denied, relative-position-only and FOV-constrained circumnavigation/enclosing, with multiple TAC/Automatica/IEEE/experimental nodes from 2012–2026.
2. **Recommend promoting B8 from provisional to stable.** Nonholonomic/unicycle, fixed-wing, multi-UAV/quadrotor and 3D dynamics form a recognizable assumption genealogy, not isolated one-off variants.
3. **Recommend refining B4 beyond “obstacle/boundary-assisted.”** Search yield is much stronger for `collision avoidance / safety / connectivity preservation / CBF / fault-tolerant reconfiguration` than for literal boundary-assisted encirclement. Possible MASTER refinement: B4a obstacle/bounded-domain; B4b safety/collision/connectivity/faults.
4. **Recommend splitting B5 conceptually during screening:** (a) **aggregate/group-target enclosure** (estimate centroid/geometric center/convex hull and surround the group) versus (b) **true target-wise allocation / subgroup / dynamic resource scheduling**. Many papers calling themselves “multi-target” solve only (a); 2026 Li et al. and Yu et al. supply stronger evidence for (b).
5. **B6 should remain, but distinguish “concurrent persistent monitoring/patrolling + encirclement” from “post-capture recovery/return-to-coverage.”** Pichierri et al. 2026 supports the former; the latter was not found as a mature classical-control subliterature in this round.
6. **New/recurrent terminology to index:** `target fencing`, `target-capturing` (often geometric, not PE), `circular formation`, `standoff tracking`, `encirclement hunting`, `whole-group encirclement`, `self-organizing fencing`, `balanced fencing`, `dynamic radius`, `relative-position-only`, `communication-free circumnavigation`.
7. **F1 boundary rule should explicitly flag lexical false friends.** “capture/hunting/interception” in titles can still be geometric formation control; classify as PE bridge only when the objective actually includes interception/reachability/adversarial escape logic.

## SEARCH GAPS
- **Largest gap: B4 literal environment constraints.** Real obstacles, bounded domains, walls/boundaries and boundary-assisted encirclement are much less covered than inter-agent collision avoidance/connectivity. Current hits mostly treat obstacles as safety constraints, not as strategic enclosure geometry.
- **B6 post-encirclement continuation/recovery remains very weak.** Pichierri et al. 2026 gives concurrent patrol+encirclement, but this round found little classical work on `encircle/capture → release/recover → coverage/search` task continuation.
- **B5 true coalition/assignment remains only partially covered.** Older “multi-target” papers often enclose a target group center/convex hull; dynamic subgroup recruitment, target-wise coalition sizing, reassignment after target loss/arrival and repeated arrivals need a dedicated follow-up.
- **Limited-FOV is clearly a real subbranch but historically shallow.** Strong recent 2024/2026 papers were found, but the backward genealogy of FOV-specific encirclement is not yet saturated.
- **Realistic clutter + local sensing + multi-target + physical UAV dynamics in one paper is still sparse.** Most papers relax one or two assumptions at a time.
- **Fixed-wing/missile “encirclement hunting” overlaps with interception guidance.** More careful SCREEN work is needed to separate geometric formation from true PE/interception objectives.
- **Citation graph coverage is partial.** Direct Google Scholar / Scopus / Web of Science forward-citation expansion was unavailable; stronger database-level forward citation checking remains for MASTER or a later search round.
- **Author/lab chains are partial, not exhaustive.** In particular, Anderson/Shames/Deghat, NUAA Jia/Zhang, Zheng/Lin/Sun, Pan/Chen and 2026 multi-target groups warrant systematic forward expansion if the branch is reopened.
