# READING LEDGER

Tracks the reading queue, completion status, and compact synthesis. Long notes belong under `notes/reading/`.

## Status vocabulary

### Screening status
- `UNSCREENED`
- `SCREENING`
- `SCREENED`

### Reading status
- `NOT_QUEUED`
- `QUEUED`
- `READING`
- `READ`
- `REVISIT`

### Decision
- `MUST READ`
- `MAP`
- `ARCHIVE`
