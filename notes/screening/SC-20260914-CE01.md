# P0003 — Kim & Sugie 2007

## Bibliographic verification
- **Title:** Cooperative control for target-capturing task based on a cyclic pursuit strategy
- **Authors:** Tae-Hyoung Kim; Toshiharu Sugie
- **Venue/year:** *Automatica* 43(8), 1426–1431, 2007
- **DOI:** `10.1016/j.automatica.2007.01.018`
- **Status:** [Web核验] Published journal article; metadata verified on ScienceDirect.

## Verified problem / task
- [原文] A group of `n` agents in 3D is driven to a prescribed geometric formation enclosing a target using modified cyclic pursuit.
- [原文] The stated `target-capturing` objective is convergence to the desired pursuit/enclosing formation; the paper does not formulate a strategic evader, capture set, winning region, or escape game.
- **Single/multi-target:** single target.
- **Capture/success definition:** geometric formation convergence, not PE capture.

## Observation
- [原文] Agent `i` measures relative vectors to the target and to one cyclic successor; the paper explicitly emphasizes local information.
- [AI判断] This is local relative measurement, but not CoCap-style intermittent enemy observability: the controller assumes the target-relative vector is available to every agent in the control law. No finite FOV, occlusion, target-loss, or detection phase is evidenced.

## Communication
- [原文] No inter-agent communication mechanism is necessary.
- [AI判断] “Communication-free” here is enabled by direct sensing of the target and one neighbor; it should not be read as solving target discovery or information propagation.

## Centralization
- [原文] Inherently distributed controller; each agent decides from its own local measurements.
- [AI判断] No hidden centralized planner/estimator was found in the screened text.

## Dynamics / action
- [原文] Each agent is an autonomous point mass with first-order position dynamics `dot p_i = u_i`; the paper explicitly states agent dynamics are not considered and full actuation is assumed.
- [AI判断] Strong geometric-control result, weak physical-UAV realism.

## Target model
- [原文] Target position enters directly as a measured relative vector.
- [AI判断] The paper is not an adversarial target model. The exact theorem-level admissible target-motion class is not important to the F1 decision but should be normalized during READ if lineage details are needed.

## Obstacles / safety
- [原文] Collision avoidance among agents is guaranteed under the proposed formation-control setting.
- No environmental-obstacle or boundary model was identified.

## Guarantee
- [原文] Global convergence to the desired formation and no inter-agent collision are claimed/proved.

## Real experiment
- [原文] Simulation examples only; no real-robot/UAV experiment.

## Key contribution
- [AI判断] Canonical historical node connecting cyclic pursuit to target-centered geometric enclosing with minimal local information and no communication.

## Major limitation
- [AI判断] The word `capturing` is a lexical false friend: the terminal objective is geometric enclosure under first-order fully actuated dynamics, not guaranteed interception of a strategic/free-escaping target.

## CoCap relevance
- **Local sensing:** partial conceptual relevance; assumes always-available target-relative measurement.
- **Multi-target allocation:** none.
- **Dynamic subgroup/reorganization:** none.
- **Persistent monitoring/coverage:** none.
- **Encirclement → interception:** no.
- **Post-capture continuation:** no.
- **Obstacles:** no environmental obstacles.
- **Real UAV dynamics:** no.

## Unresolved evidence
- `NEEDS_FULLTEXT` only if MASTER wants the exact admissible target-motion assumptions in the convergence theorem. It is **not** needed to resolve the main screening decision.

## Recommended decision
**MUST READ.**

**Reason:** [AI判断] Not because it is CoCap-near, but because it is a canonical genealogy root and an essential terminology correction: it establishes what a major classical `target-capturing` paper actually guarantees and what it does not.

- [原文] The task is split into far-range approach and close-range coordinated enclosing.
- **Single/multi-target:** single fixed target.
- **Capture/success definition:** uniform circular enclosing motion, not interception.

## Observation
- [原文] Close-range control uses locally available relative distances and bearing angles.
- [原文] Sensing range is explicitly limited: a far robot may not sense the target; once a robot is in the target-visible close region, the controller keeps it from losing target visibility.
- [AI判断] This is genuinely relevant local-sensing structure, but it is **not a complete search/detection solution**. The paper treats navigation from outside the sensing region as a separate subproblem and discusses existing methods for that step.

## Communication
- [原文] The motivation explicitly includes local sensors and limited communication; the enclosing law is local/distributed and interaction topology can depend on robot states.
- [AI判断] Exact message content is less central than the local relative sensing assumption, but should be normalized during READ if used as a communication baseline.

## Centralization
- [原文] Decentralized/distributed solution; robots are indistinguishable and no preassigned global interaction ordering is required in the same sense as cyclic-pursuit predecessors.

## Dynamics / action
- [原文] Planar unicycle kinematics with nonholonomic pure-rolling/non-slipping model; controller acts through the unicycle motion inputs.

## Target model
- [原文] Fixed target.
- [AI判断] No strategic escape behavior.

## Obstacles / safety
- No environmental-obstacle treatment was established for the proposed controller in the screened evidence.
- No CBF/hard obstacle-safety layer.

## Guarantee
- [原文] Reachability and invariance analysis provides an attraction-domain result and essentially global convergence to the desired evenly spaced circular motion under the stated hybrid-control setup.
- [原文] Target visibility is invariant once the robot is in the close sensing region.

## Real experiment
- [原文] Simulation of five unicycles; no physical experiment identified.

## Key contribution
- [AI判断] Canonical bridge from abstract point-mass cyclic pursuit to nonholonomic local-information enclosing, with explicit finite sensing range and reachability/invariance reasoning.

## Major limitation
- [AI判断] Its strongest “local sensing” result is conditional on getting into sensing range; it does not solve unknown-target exploration, occlusion, adversarial target loss, or moving-target pursuit.

## CoCap relevance

- **Multi-target allocation:** none.
- **Dynamic subgroup/reorganization:** no target-wise role switching.
- **Persistent monitoring/coverage:** no.
- **Encirclement → interception:** no.
- **Post-capture continuation:** no.
- **Obstacles:** absent from demonstrated controller.
- **Real UAV dynamics:** no; unicycle model only.

## Unresolved evidence

## Verified problem / task
- [原文] Multi-robot encirclement of a **moving target in 3D**; three variants of the encirclement problem are treated.
- **Single/multi-target:** single moving target.
- **Capture/success definition:** encirclement geometry; no PE winning/capture event established.

## Observation
- [原文] The method is decentralized and each robot **locally estimates all relevant global quantities**.
- [AI判断] This is the largest assumption surprise in the paper: local communication/decentralized computation does **not** mean each robot acts only on raw local partial target observations. A distributed estimator reconstructs global quantities used by the controller.

## Communication
- [原文] Requires local communication among robots.
- [AI判断] Therefore it is not communication-free, and its local-information semantics are weaker than a strict “unseen target stays unknown except through bounded neighbor messages” interpretation.

## Centralization
- [原文] No centralized controller is required; estimation/control is decentralized.
- [AI判断] Functionally global state-like quantities are nevertheless recovered locally by estimation, so “decentralized” and “partial observability” must remain separate taxonomy axes.

## Dynamics / action
- [原文] Theory/simulation includes kinematic point robots; quadrotor UAVs appear in simulation; hardware experiments use differential-drive wheeled mobile robots.
- [AI判断] The theorem-level model should not be conflated with real UAV dynamics.

## Target model
- [原文] Moving 3D target.
- [AI判断] No strategic evader/winning-region formulation was identified.

## Obstacles / safety
- [原文] Extension guarantees maintenance of safe **inter-robot distance**.
- [AI判断] This is collision safety, not evidence of cluttered-environment obstacle avoidance or boundary-assisted capture.

## Guarantee
- [原文] Effectiveness of the encirclement variants is formally established; safe inter-robot separation is guaranteed in the safety extension.

## Real experiment
- [原文/Web核验] Yes, on differential-drive wheeled mobile robots.
- [原文] Quadrotor UAV validation is simulation, not real-UAV flight.

## Key contribution
- [AI判断] Strong classical realism node: 3D moving target + decentralized estimation/control + formal collision separation + physical multi-robot experiment.

## Major limitation
- [AI判断] Its strongest decentralization claim relies on local estimation of globally relevant quantities. This is materially easier/different than maintaining robust behavior under genuinely incomplete and intermittent enemy observability.

## CoCap relevance
- **Local sensing:** medium; estimator-mediated rather than strict raw local enemy observability.
- **Multi-target allocation:** none.
- **Dynamic subgroup/reorganization:** no target-wise subgroup policy.
- **Persistent monitoring/coverage:** no.
- **Encirclement → interception:** no.
- **Post-capture continuation:** no.
- **Obstacles:** only inter-robot collision guarantee in screened evidence.
- **Real UAV dynamics:** no real UAV; UAV simulation only.

## Unresolved evidence
`NEEDS_FULLTEXT` for:
1. exact sensor inputs to the target/localization layer;
2. exact neighbor message content/range/graph assumptions;
3. whether any target quantity is directly available to all robots versus propagated by the estimator;
4. exact theorem-level actuation constraints.

## Recommended decision
**MUST READ.**

**Reason:** [AI判断] It is a canonical realism/decentralization node and directly prevents a false novelty comparison: “decentralized” classical encirclement can still reconstruct globally relevant state through distributed estimation.

# P0011 — Li et al. 2026

## Bibliographic verification
- **Title:** Dynamic Task Allocation and Whole-Group Encirclement of Multiple Targets in Multi-Agent Swarms via Voronoi Partitioning
- **Authors:** Chenyang Li; Yonghui Yang; Yilin Han; Xue-Bo Chen; Tianxin Zhao
- **Venue/status:** *Mathematics and Computers in Simulation*, available online 26 Aug 2026, in press / journal pre-proof

- **Status:** [Web核验] ScienceDirect online-first metadata verified. Secondary future issue-year metadata should not replace the online-first 2026 status yet.

## Verified problem / task
- [原文] Multiple moving targets are modeled as **one integrated target group**. Outermost targets generate encirclement points; agents progressively form an enclosure around the **entire target group**.
- [原文] Voronoi cells and regional load are then used to dynamically reassign agents among encirclement points/regions.
- **Critical correction:** [AI判断] This is **not target-wise allocation of subgroups to different targets**. It is whole-group enclosure plus resource balancing over encirclement points.
- **Capture/success definition:** stable whole-group enclosure / resource-balanced encirclement, not individual target capture.

## Observation
- [原文] The method explicitly requires identifying the outermost targets and constructing the polygon/group boundary used for encirclement-point generation.
- [AI判断] Whether each agent obtains that group-level geometry from global target states or by a distributed estimator is decision-critical and not resolved by the accessible sections.

## Communication
- [原文] Graph-theoretic local interaction topologies are part of the formulation.
- Exact message contents and whether outermost-target/global Voronoi information is locally reconstructible remain unresolved.

## Centralization
- [AI判断] The controller is framed in a multi-agent/distributed-control context, but **do not label it fully decentralized under local target observability yet**. Whole-group outer-hull construction may hide a global-information requirement unless the full implementation proves otherwise.

## Dynamics / action
- [原文] Agent and target dynamics/control inputs are modeled for controller/stability analysis; the method is potential/Voronoi-based rather than a realistic UAV flight-control interface.
- Exact order/input limits should be checked only if needed later.

## Target model
- [原文] Multiple moving targets form an integrated group; the method also accounts for how agents may disturb target motion.
- [AI判断] “target escape” terminology does not establish a strategic PE evader or minimax escape policy.

## Obstacles / safety
- [原文] The polygon formed by outermost targets is a **restricted area**; a repulsive potential prevents agents from crossing the target-group interior, and a correction term mitigates deadlock.
- [原文] Stability section also addresses collision prevention.
- [AI判断] The restricted target-group polygon is not an environmental obstacle map and should not be counted as clutter/obstacle-assisted encirclement.

## Guarantee
- [原文] Lyapunov stability analysis; screened sections describe collision prevention, velocity consensus within connected components, and convergence toward stable group structures.

## Real experiment
- [原文] Extensive simulations; no physical robot/UAV experiment reported in the accessible publisher record.

## Key contribution
- [AI判断] A useful modern **whole-group multi-target enclosure + dynamic encirclement-point load balancing** method.

## Major limitation
- [AI判断] The title can be badly misleading for CoCap mapping. Its “dynamic task allocation” is not dynamic recruitment of capture subgroups per target and not post-capture reassignment among surviving targets.

## CoCap relevance
- **Local sensing:** unresolved/global-geometry risk.
- **Multi-target allocation:** related only at a resource-balancing level; **not target-wise coalition allocation**.
- **Dynamic subgroup/reorganization:** agents switch encirclement points/regions, not target-specific capture coalitions.
- **Persistent monitoring/coverage:** no.
- **Encirclement → interception:** no.
- **Post-capture continuation:** no.
- **Obstacles:** no real environmental obstacle model evidenced.
- **Real UAV dynamics:** no.

## Unresolved evidence
`NEEDS_FULLTEXT` for:
1. exact information required to identify outermost targets / group polygon;
2. whether group geometry is global, centralized, broadcast, or reconstructible by local exchange;
3. communication graph/message contents and failure modes;
4. exact target behavioral/control model if MASTER wants to compare “escape” semantics.

## Recommended decision
**MAP.**

**Reason:** [AI判断] Important taxonomy correction and a valid strong alternative for whole-group enclosure/resource balancing, but it does not occupy the key CoCap node of target-wise allocation + dynamic subgroup recruitment. It therefore does not justify deep reading before stronger allocation/PE papers.

# Structural answers for MASTER

## 1. What has classical encirclement already solved?

[AI判断] Classical encirclement is much more mature than “formation around a known point” alone. Across this batch it already covers: distributed/local relative measurements; finite sensing-range behavior; nonholonomic robots; moving and 3D targets; distributed estimators; explicit FOV constraints; inter-agent collision guarantees; CBF obstacle safety; physical ground/aerial experiments; persistent concurrent patrol+encirclement; and a recent encirclement→interception mode transition.

[AI判断] What it still does **not** solve as one mature package is: strategic adversarial capturability under local/incomplete sensing; target-wise multi-target coalition allocation; detection-triggered recruitment while nonparticipants keep coverage; repeated captures/arrivals; explicit post-capture recovery/resumption; and clutter/boundary geometry used as a strategic capture resource.

## 2. What does `multi-target encirclement` mean here?

[原文] P0011 explicitly models multiple targets as **an integrated target group**, identifies outermost targets, and encircles the entire group. Its dynamic allocation is among generated **encirclement points/regions**.

[AI判断] Therefore P0011 is **not** evidence that classical control already solves CoCap-style target-wise assignment or dynamic subteam coalition recruitment. The canonical map should not describe it as “target-wise allocation frontier” without qualification.

## 3. Is `local sensing` actually local observability?

Three distinct cases appear and should be separated:
1. **Direct always-available local relative measurement** — P0003.
2. **Literal finite sensing region / visibility maintenance** — P0004; but target acquisition outside sensing is delegated to a separate navigation problem.
3. **Distributed estimation reconstructing globally relevant quantities** — P0008.

P0009/P0012 add FOV/range-limited perception, but exact sensor dropout/data-fusion topology still needs full text.

[AI判断] Thus `distributed`, `decentralized`, `local information`, `limited sensing`, and `partial observability` must never be a single binary taxonomy field.

## 4. Which capture/hunting/fencing/interception terms are false friends?

- **P0003 `target-capturing`** — [AI判断] geometric enclosing; false friend for PE capture.
- **P0004 `capture` wording** — [AI判断] uniform circular enclosing around fixed target; false friend.
- **P0011 `target escape` / multi-target encirclement** — [AI判断] no evidence of strategic PE capture; escape wording does not create a game-theoretic capture result.
- **P0012 `interception/neutralization`** — [AI判断] **not merely lexical**; it is a real task-mode transition to active interception, although not yet verified as a minimax/PE capturability theorem.

## 5. Is there a mature `encircle → complete task → resume coverage/search` classical lineage?

**No such mature lineage was established by this screening batch.**

[原文] P0010 performs encirclement and patrol/monitoring **concurrently**.

[AI判断] Concurrent optimization is not equivalent to CoCap's desired phase semantics `coverage/search → detection → recruit/capture → post-capture release/recovery → resume coverage`, especially when new/repeated targets may arrive. This gap remains credible but should still be phrased as **undercovered**, not proven absent.

## 6. Is P0012 a genuine F1 bridge?

**Yes, with a narrower label.**

[原文] It explicitly transitions from protected-target encirclement to encircling/intercepting/neutralizing a hostile moving target using range-based state estimation and an anti-target controller, with real UAV validation.

[AI判断] Recommended label: **functional encirclement → interception bridge**. Do **not** yet label it a full geometric-encirclement → PE-winning/capturability bridge, because the screened theory establishes observability/estimation and encirclement convergence, while the hostile target strategy and exact interception terminal condition remain unresolved.

# Taxonomy changes recommended

1. **Split local-information semantics** into at least:
   - direct local relative sensing;
   - finite-range/FOV sensing with target loss;
   - distributed estimation that reconstructs global target/team quantities;
   - globally shared/broadcast target state.
2. **Refine B5 multi-target** into:
   - aggregate/group-target enclosure;
   - target-wise independent enclosure;
   - target-wise agent/subgroup allocation;
   - encirclement-point/region resource balancing.
   P0011 belongs primarily to the first and fourth categories.
3. **Refine B6 persistent tasks** into:
   - concurrent patrol/monitor + encirclement;
   - sequential search/coverage → encirclement/capture;
   - post-capture release/recovery → resume coverage;
   - repeated-arrival/reassignment loop.
4. **Refine B4 safety/environment** into:
   - inter-agent collision avoidance;
   - external obstacle hard safety;
   - connectivity preservation;
   - fault-tolerant reconfiguration;
   - strategic boundary/obstacle-assisted capture geometry.
5. **Refine F1 bridge strength** into:
   - lexical false friend (`capture/hunt/fence` but only formation);
   - functional interception bridge (P0012);
   - formal PE capture/capturability bridge (winning region/barrier/capture certificate).
6. For physical realism, record **theorem model / simulation plant / real-hardware plant separately**. P0008 demonstrates why a single “UAV” tag is misleading.
