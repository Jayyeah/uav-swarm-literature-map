`Isaacs / differential games`

→ `reach-avoid = reach target while satisfying avoid/state constraints` (P0019)

## P0016 — Multiplayer reach-avoid differential games with simple motions: A review

### Bibliographic identity
- Rui Yan, Ruiliang Deng, Xiaoming Duan, Zongying Shi, Yisheng Zhong, Frontiers in Control Engineering, 2023 (Vol. 3 / 2022 article record).
- DOI: `10.3389/fcteg.2022.1093186`.
- [Web核验] Publisher full article verified.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Two adversarial teams play in a region split into **goal region + play region**. Evaders start in the play region and try to enter the goal region; pursuers protect the goal region by capturing evaders before entry.
- [原文] The review explicitly covers **games of kind and games of degree** and connects small-scale barrier/winning-region analysis to team-level task allocation.
- [原文] Evader exits by capture in the play region, capture at the splitting hypersurface, or successful entry into the goal region.

### Dynamics
- [原文] Focus is **simple motion / first-order integrator with bounded speed**, with instantaneous heading change.
- [原文] Review baseline assumes pursuers are at least as fast as evaders.
- [原文] Capture can be radius capture or point capture; capture radius is non-negative.

### Information pattern
- [原文] Baseline assumption is **full-state information**: player positions and speeds are known.
- [原文] The review itself identifies incomplete/partial information as a limitation and future direction.

### Environment
- [原文] Reach-avoid region contains goal/play structure; reviewed analytical results often specialize further to planar bounded/convex geometries.
- [AI判断] “Simple motion review” should not be generalized to unknown/nonconvex clutter or local-FOV UAV operation.

### Multiplayer structure / allocation
- [原文] Individual pursuers may be unassigned, act alone, or form a coalition. Task allocation asks which coalition can capture which evader before goal entry, then solves a matching/ILP-type problem.
- [原文] Barrier/winning-region construction is the key primitive for deciding whether an assignment edge is feasible.

### Solution objects
- [原文] The review carefully treats **barrier**, **winning region**, and **dominance region** as related but distinct objects; dominance is tied to who can reach points first under simple motion, while barrier separates winning regions of the game.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Best batch-level organizer for the analytical-barrier + assignment lineage. It explicitly places P0018/P0019-style HJI tools alongside geometric methods and then shows why multiplayer work decomposes into low-cardinality certificates plus assignment.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] High. It supplies the abstraction CoCap needs most: **a pursuer/coalition–target feasibility certificate as an input to allocation**. The direct theorem assumptions, however, are much stronger than CoCap’s local sensing and realistic motion.

### Limitations
- [原文] Simple motion, full state, known speed bounds, mostly fixed player populations, and limited treatment of more realistic dynamics/information patterns.
- [AI判断] These are exactly the dimensions in which a hard “guaranteed capturable” edge becomes unreliable in CoCap and may need to become a learned/probabilistic/robust score.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — strongest compact organizer for the exact theoretical branch MASTER is trying to build.

## P0018 — A Time-Dependent Hamilton-Jacobi Formulation of Reachable Sets for Continuous Dynamic Games

### Bibliographic identity
- Ian M. Mitchell, Alexandre M. Bayen, Claire J. Tomlin, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2005.
- DOI: `10.1109/TAC.2005.851439`.
- [Web核验] IEEE record / author manuscript verified.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Generic continuous dynamic game with a target set represented as a level set. One player attempts to steer the state away from the target; the opponent attempts to force it into the target.
- [AI判断] Its main object is structurally a **game-of-kind reachability question**: from which initial states can target entry be forced within the time horizon?
- [原文] It is **not yet a reach-avoid objective** in the P0019 sense: the primary construction is target reachability, not “reach target while simultaneously remaining outside a separate avoid/state-constraint set.”

### Dynamics
- [原文] General nonlinear ODE `xdot = f(x,a,b)` with compact input sets and regularity assumptions; not restricted to simple motion.

### Information pattern
- [原文] The forcing player is formalized with a **non-anticipative strategy** against the other player’s measurable input. The result is therefore tied to a particular lower/upper-game information convention, not an information-free geometric statement.

### Terminal / target definition
- [原文] A closed target set is represented as the zero sublevel set of a function. The backward reachable set contains states from which the forcing player can ensure that the trajectory hits the target at some time in the finite horizon.

### Solution object
- [原文] **Backward reachable set (BRS)** / reachable set in joint state space.
- [原文] Main result: the reachable set is obtained from the zero sublevel set of the viscosity solution of a time-dependent HJI PDE.
- [AI判断] This is not automatically a capture region, dominance region, or reach-avoid set. Those names require additional problem structure.

### Environment / cardinality
- [原文] Generic state-space formulation; physical obstacles are not a primitive of the main theorem unless encoded into the dynamics/target formulation.
- [AI判断] Conceptually 2-player / controller-vs-disturbance input structure; not a native NvM team solution.

### Scalability
- [原文] Grid-based HJ methods face the curse of dimensionality; the computational burden grows rapidly with state dimension.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Canonical computational turning point: Isaacs/HJI theory → numerically representable reachable sets, which later reach-avoid and pairwise decomposition papers inherit.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] A signed HJI value or BRS membership is a principled **capturability / escape feasibility score**. Direct online use for many UAVs is unrealistic, but offline pairwise/low-dimensional certificates could become features, labels, curriculum gates, or safety/allocation priors.

### Limitations
- [AI判断] Full model knowledge, continuous state, selected non-anticipative information structure, and state-dimensional scaling prevent direct transplantation to local-sensing multi-UAV CoCap.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — historical/methodological root of the computational reachability branch.

## P0019 — Hamilton–Jacobi Formulation for Reach–Avoid Differential Games

### Bibliographic identity
- Kostas Margellos, John Lygeros, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2011.
- DOI: `10.1109/TAC.2011.2105730`; arXiv: `0911.4625`.
- [Web核验] IEEE/arXiv/author manuscript verified.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Competing-input nonlinear dynamic game with **target hitting plus state constraints**.
- [原文] It considers both terminal-time and any-time reach formulations: reach a target while satisfying the required state/avoid constraints up to the relevant time.
- [AI判断] This is the substantive step beyond P0018: **reachability is conjuncted with avoidance/viability until the target is reached**.

### Dynamics
- [原文] General nonlinear dynamics with compact competing input sets and regularity assumptions. Not restricted to simple motion.
- [原文] Demonstration includes an underactuated vehicle/aircraft-style scenario with environmental disturbance and spatial/temporal constraints.

### Information pattern
- [原文] Uses a non-anticipative-strategy formulation for the competing inputs.
- [AI判断] As with P0018, the guarantee is strategy-class dependent and cannot be read as a purely geometric full-information fact.

### Capture / reach-avoid definition
- [原文] The desired set is reached while trajectories satisfy state constraints / avoid forbidden conditions before reaching it.
- [AI判断] A **reach-avoid set** is therefore not synonymous with the P0018 BRS: it encodes both reach and avoid predicates.

### Solution object / guarantee
- [原文] Continuous value-function / Hamilton–Jacobi characterization of the reach-avoid game with numerical advantages over formulations that induce discontinuous Hamiltonians/value functions.
- [AI判断] This provides the right conceptual object for “can a target reach safety before being captured?” or its defender dual.

### Environment / cardinality / scalability
- [原文] State constraints can encode obstacles or unsafe conditions.
- [AI判断] Still fundamentally a low-dimensional dynamic-game formulation; direct joint-state NvM use suffers dimensional explosion.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] P0018 → P0019 is a genuine conceptual extension: **target reachability → target reachability under avoidance/state constraints**. It is not merely a renaming of the same reachable set.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] High for escape-gap blocking: an evader’s objective is closer to “reach an escape/target region while avoiding pursuers” than to unconstrained target reachability. A robust approximate reach-avoid margin could be a stronger allocation/support signal than distance alone.

### Limitations
- [AI判断] Known dynamics/state constraints and non-anticipative information assumptions remain far from local, noisy, intermittent enemy sensing.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — canonical reach-avoid formulation and necessary terminology anchor.

## P0020 — Pursuit–evasion games in the presence of obstacles

### Bibliographic identity
- Dave W. Oyler, Pierre T. Kabamba, Anouck R. Girard, Automatica 65 (2016), 1–11.
- DOI: `10.1016/j.automatica.2015.11.018`.
- [Web核验] ScienceDirect reports 174 `Cited by` at the 2026-09-14 SEARCH snapshot.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Planar pursuit-evasion with players moving at fixed, not necessarily equal, speeds; pursuer capture requires zero separation (point capture); evader avoids capture.
- [原文] The paper explicitly discusses both **games of kind** (who can guarantee victory/capture) and **games of degree** (e.g. capture time).
- [原文] A second three-player predator/protector/prey-style game shows how the same dominance machinery can solve a multiplayer rescue-type objective.

### Dynamics / information
- [原文] **Simple motion**, instantaneous turning, constant speed.
- [原文] Each player knows the opponent’s location at all times: full-state positional information for the modeled game.

### Environment
- [原文] Known line-segment and polygonal obstacles; also obstacles with **asymmetric effects** on different players.

### Solution object
- [原文] **Dominance region**: points in the plane a player can reach before the opponent regardless of the opponent’s actions.
- [原文] Dominance boundaries are built from travel-time/isochrone geometry in the obstacle domain; the paper shows dominance analysis can give a complete solution for its modeled games.
- [AI判断] Dominance region is a physical-space race-to-point object, not automatically the same as a joint-state winning/reach-avoid set.

### What obstacles change
- [原文] With no obstacle and simple motion, the familiar boundary is Apollonius-like. With obstacles, shortest-path geometry changes the boundary; obstacles can enlarge or shrink a player’s dominance region depending on geometry and can affect the players asymmetrically.
- [AI判断] Therefore “compute Euclidean Apollonius circle, then add collision avoidance” is not generally valid. The obstacle changes the **strategic interception geometry**, not only feasibility of a nominal path.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Key correction branch from clean Euclidean dominance geometry to constrained environments.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] Strong for obstacle/boundary-aware escape blocking. A useful baseline is geodesic/intercept-time dominance rather than raw Euclidean angle/distance when walls or no-fly regions matter.

### Limitations
- [原文] Simple motion, known map, full positions, deterministic fixed speeds, point capture.
- [AI判断] CoCap’s unknown/local perception and UAV acceleration/yaw dynamics break the exact geometry.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — decisive assumption correction for any CoCap claim involving obstacles/boundaries as strategic capture geometry.

## P0021 — One Apollonius Circle is Enough for Many Pursuit-Evasion Games

### Bibliographic identity
- Michael Dorothy, Dipankar Maity, Daigo Shishika, Alexander Von Moll, Automatica 163 (2024), 111587.
- DOI: `10.1016/j.automatica.2024.111587`; arXiv: `2111.09205`.
- [Web核验] ScienceDirect reports 58 `Cited by` at the 2026-09-14 SEARCH snapshot.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] 1 pursuer vs 1 evader, **obstacle-free, full-information, simple-motion**, pursuer faster than evader, **point capture** terminal event.
- No defended target region is required by the main theorem.

### Solution object
- [原文] The initial **Apollonius circle (AC)** and its interior, identified as the evader’s dominance region under these assumptions.
- [原文] The paper constructs a pursuer strategy that guarantees capture in an arbitrarily small neighborhood of the **initial** AC against any admissible evader strategy.
- [原文] Consequence: the evader may choose the capture location freely only within the initial AC; location-of-capture payoff games become substantially simpler.

### What the AC guarantees
- [AI判断] Under the exact assumptions, it provides a **capture-location / evader-accessible-region certificate**, while faster-pursuer dynamics already guarantee eventual capture.
- [AI判断] It does **not** by itself certify: obstacle-rich pursuit, positive capture radius, nonholonomic motion, partial/local sensing, NvM team capture, or a reach-avoid target objective.

### Game of kind / degree
- [AI判断] The binary capture question is a game of kind, but the paper’s distinctive value is stronger for a class of **games of degree whose payoff depends on capture location**.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Precision/correction node for Apollonius reasoning. It clarifies the exact status of the initial AC rather than creating a universal PE winning set.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] Useful as a cheap geometric prior for pairwise interception or escape-sector features when simple-motion assumptions approximately hold. It should never be treated as a hard guarantee once obstacles/local sensing/UAV dynamics are introduced.

### Limitations
- [原文] Restrictive assumptions are explicit: obstacle-free, full information, simple motion, faster pursuer, point capture.

### Recommendation
- **MAP** — theorem is important and compact, but SCREEN already resolves its exact scope; it is not in the minimum proof-level reading set unless CoCap adopts an Apollonius-based baseline.

## P0024 — Multiplayer Reach-Avoid Games via Pairwise Outcomes

### Bibliographic identity
- Mo Chen, Zhengyuan Zhou, Claire J. Tomlin, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 62(3), 2017.
- DOI: `10.1109/TAC.2016.2577619`; arXiv: `1611.00450`.
- [Web核验] IEEE/arXiv publication identity verified.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] `N_A` attackers and `N_D` defenders move in a compact/bounded planar domain with obstacles. Attackers seek to send a prescribed `M` attackers to a compact target set; defenders prevent this by **capture or indefinite delay**.
- [原文] Capture occurs within a positive/non-negative radius `R_C` in the pairwise state.
- [AI判断] The yes/no question “can M attackers reach the target?” is a game-of-kind question; “how many attackers can defenders prevent?” is a team performance/cardinality question.

### Dynamics
- [原文] Decoupled planar first-order/simple-motion dynamics with player-specific maximum speeds and control direction in the unit disk.
- [原文] The 2D path-defense approximation additionally assumes the defender is not slower than the attacker.

### Information pattern
- [原文] The 4D HJI pairwise solution yields **closed-loop optimal** controls from the reach-avoid value function.
- [AI判断] The paper is not a limited-sensing formulation; pairwise state and model information are available to construct/update the outcome matrix.

### Solution object
- [原文] Pairwise `1v1` **reach-avoid set** partitions pairwise joint state into attacker-winning vs defender-winning states.
- [原文] Two pairwise solvers are used: a 4D HJI solution and a more scalable 2D path-defense approximation.

### Multiplayer extension
- [原文] Compute `N_A N_D` pairwise outcomes, construct a bipartite feasibility graph, then use **maximum matching** to merge them into a multiplayer assignment.
- [原文] The paper states defender performance guarantees; the 2D path-defense version is deliberately conservative toward the defender while scaling better.
- [AI判断] Crucial semantic correction: pairwise winning edges + matching provide a **team-level certificate/lower-bound style guarantee**, not a proof that the full high-dimensional NvM differential game has been solved optimally.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Strongest batch example of `low-dimensional HJI/reach-avoid certificate → combinatorial assignment`.
- [AI判断] It inherits computational HJI reachability ideas from the P0018 lineage and applies them to a reach-avoid objective; it is not simply “HJI at team dimension.”

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] Very high. Replace “distance-to-target” assignment with a matrix `C_ij` indicating whether pursuer i can guarantee/likely intercept target j before escape; then matching gives a principled allocation baseline. A signed HJI value can further provide a margin rather than a binary edge.
- [AI判断] For support recruitment, the natural generalization is to add coalition certificates `C_{S,j}` or marginal improvement of adding supporter k to target j.

### Limitations
- [原文] Known compact map/obstacles, simple motion, known maximum speeds, pairwise state/model availability.
- [AI判断] Independent pairwise certificates do not automatically survive local sensing, simultaneous coupling/collisions, nonholonomic UAV dynamics, or changing/repeated target arrivals.

### Uncertainty
- `NEEDS_FULLTEXT`: before using P0024 as a formal theorem baseline, verify the exact theorem statement translating maximum-matching cardinality into the defender’s guaranteed number of stopped attackers and the assumptions under which simultaneous pairwise assignments do not invalidate that bound. SCREEN establishes the direction and guarantee type, not the proof details.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — strongest PE→allocation bridge in the numerical/HJI branch.

## P0025 — Task Assignment for Multiplayer Reach-Avoid Games in Convex Domains via Analytical Barriers

### Bibliographic identity
- Rui Yan, Zongying Shi, Yisheng Zhong, IEEE Transactions on Robotics 36(1), 2020.
- DOI: `10.1109/TRO.2019.2935345`; arXiv: `1904.02789`.
- [Web核验] IEEE/arXiv formal publication verified.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Two adversarial teams in a **bounded convex planar domain** partitioned by a straight target line into play region and target region. Evaders try to enter the target region; pursuers try to capture them first.
- [原文] **Point capture**: pursuer and evader positions coincide in the play region.
- [原文] Authors explicitly describe the central analysis as a **game of kind**: whether a pursuit coalition can guarantee capture before target entry.

### Dynamics / speed
- [原文] All players are mass points with first-order/simple-motion dynamics.
- [原文] Homogeneous pursuer speed `v_P`, homogeneous evader speed `v_E`, with `alpha = v_E/v_P`, and the paper focuses on **faster pursuers: 0 < alpha < 1**.

### Information pattern
- [原文] The paper focuses on a **non-anticipative information structure**. Pursuit decisions use state-feedback information and the evasion team’s current input under the stated Stackelberg convention; the paper explicitly notes the information asymmetry.
- [AI判断] This is substantially stronger than CoCap’s local sensing and should not be labeled merely “full-state feedback.” The Stackelberg/non-anticipative convention is part of the guarantee.

### Solution object
- [原文] Analytical **barrier** separates Pursuit Winning Region (PWR) from Evasion Winning Region (EWR). PWR means the coalition can guarantee capture before target entry; EWR means the evader can reach the target regardless of pursuit strategy.
- [原文] Barriers are constructed analytically for one-pursuer/one-evader and multi-pursuer/one-evader coalitions under stated deployment assumptions.

### Multiplayer assignment
- [原文] For each coalition–evader pair, the barrier produces a binary prior feasibility value: can this coalition guarantee capture before target entry?
- [原文] The resulting prior-information vector is the input to a simplified **0–1 integer program / matching** that maximizes the number of evaders guaranteed intercepted.
- [原文] Within the paper’s modeled class, a key lemma allows any useful capture coalition to be reduced to a coalition of at most two pursuers for the maximum matching; this drastically reduces the assignment search.
- [AI判断] The “at most two pursuers” result is **not a general truth about pursuit**. It depends on this simple-motion, convex-domain, faster-pursuer, point-capture, analytical-barrier setup.

### Genealogy role
- [AI判断] Strongest batch example of `analytical barrier / winning-region certificate → coalition feasibility matrix → integer task assignment`.
- [AI判断] P0025 should not be described as merely “P0024 with an analytic solver.” It adds explicit coalition capture certificates and a different information/domain assumption set. The two are sibling allocation branches sharing the same certificate→matching philosophy.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] Extremely high. It suggests a clean support-recruitment primitive: for target j, compute whether `{i}`, `{i,k}`, ... cross a capturability barrier; recruit the **smallest coalition whose certificate is positive**, then free surplus agents for coverage/search.
- [AI判断] A practical CoCap analogue could replace exact analytical barriers with learned/robust capture margins while preserving the combinatorial allocation layer.

### Limitations
- [原文] Convex known planar domain, straight target boundary, point mass/simple motion, homogeneous team speeds, faster pursuers, point capture, strong non-anticipative/Stackelberg information structure.
- [AI判断] These assumptions are far from local FOV, unknown target control law, obstacles/occlusion, UAV acceleration limits, and online changing coalitions.

### Uncertainty
- `NEEDS_FULLTEXT`: if MASTER adopts P0025 as a formal baseline, verify the exact scope of Assumptions 2/3 used for the multi-pursuer barrier and the global-optimality statement of the reduced assignment. SCREEN has verified the stated reduction and assignment mechanism, not reconstructed its proof.

### Recommendation
- **MUST READ** — strongest PE→support-recruitment / coalition-allocation bridge in the analytical branch.

## P0028 — Cooperative Pursuit With Multi-Pursuer and One Faster Free-Moving Evader

### Bibliographic identity
- Xu Fang, Chen Wang, Lihua Xie, Jie Chen, IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics 52(3), 2022 print / online 2020.
- DOI: `10.1109/TCYB.2019.2958548`; arXiv: `2001.04731`.
- [Web核验] PubMed/SUNY formal publication metadata verified; SEARCH snapshot records 152 Scopus citations via SUNY Pure on 2026-09-14.

### Verified game definition
- [原文] Multiple pursuers versus **one faster, freely moving evader**. The evader’s moving direction/escape strategy is not known in advance and is not constrained to a fixed path or limited escape direction.
- [原文] Capture is **positive-radius capture**: distance to a pursuer becomes smaller than a non-zero `d_c`.
- [AI判断] The principal theorem is a **game-of-kind sufficient condition**: under specified initial geometry/speed/distance conditions, capture is guaranteed regardless of the evader strategy.

### Dynamics / speed
- [原文] Pursuers are planar single integrators with heterogeneous maximum speeds `V_i`; the evader is faster than the pursuers.
- [原文] The controller does not assume a predictive kinematic model or known future escape strategy for the evader, but its geometric construction uses the pursuer/evader maximum-speed ratios.

### Information pattern / communication
- [原文] Each pursuer accesses position information of **the evader, itself, and its two nearest angular neighbors**. The encirclement controller is distributed with respect to this local pursuer-neighbor structure.
- [AI判断] This is much closer to CoCap than full-team global state, but it still assumes every participating pursuer knows the evader position continuously; it is therefore **not CoCap-style intermittent local enemy observability**.

### Environment
- [原文] Open planar geometry; no strategic obstacles in the theorem.

### Geometry / solution object
- [原文] Each pursuer induces an **occupied angle** determined by its speed ratio. Adjacent pursuers can have overlapping occupied angle or a positive **escapable angle**.
- [原文] Group occupied angle `theta_G` measures the union of blocked free-moving directions; `theta_E = 2pi - theta_G` is the total escape-angle measure.
- [原文] `theta_G = 2pi` means no free-moving direction remains uncovered by the pursuer group’s capture geometry.
- [AI判断] This is an **escape-direction certificate**, not an HJI reach-avoid set and not an Apollonius winning region.

### When encirclement really implies capture
- [原文] Merely surrounding the evader is insufficient. The paper combines an angular encirclement controller with a radial “hunting” component and distance-maintenance/collision-avoidance terms.
- [原文] Theorem 2 assumes the faster evader starts **inside an n-pursuer polygon** and imposes edge-distance/safety conditions, including an upper bound tied to `2 d_c lambda_min`; under these conditions the group occupied angle remains `2pi` and the evader is eventually captured regardless of its strategy.
- [原文] The paper also derives a lower-bound style requirement on pursuer cardinality from capture radius, polygon radius, and minimum speed ratio.
- [AI判断] Therefore the valid bridge is:
  `encirclement geometry + no escape-angle gap + maintained polygon/distance condition + positive capture radius + inward approach → guaranteed capture`.
  It is **not** `ring formation → capture`.

### CoCap relevance
- [AI判断] Very high and unusually direct:
  1. `theta_E` is a candidate **escape-gap metric** for reward, diagnostics, or support recruitment;
  2. support can be recruited until the largest/total escape gap closes;
  3. agents can split into **surrounding vs hunting/approach effort**, closely matching the idea that not every supporter has the same immediate role;
  4. the theorem makes cardinality and speed ratio explicit rather than assuming “more agents is better.”

### Limitations
- [原文] Single evader, open plane, single-integrator pursuers, known speed ratios, continuous evader position for each pursuer, positive capture radius, specific initial polygon/distance conditions for the strongest theorem.

# Strongest PE → allocation bridge

[AI判断] The strongest reusable structure is a **certificate matrix followed by combinatorial allocation**:

1. For pursuer `i` and target `j`, compute a pairwise capture/reach-avoid certificate `C_ij` (P0024), or for coalition `S` and target `j`, `C_{S,j}` (P0025).
2. Treat `C=1` as “this resource unit can guarantee interception under the model” rather than as “this agent is close.”
3. Solve maximum matching / 0–1 assignment to maximize the number of targets that are certifiably handled.
4. For CoCap, relax exact binary guarantees into a **capture margin** (signed HJI value, barrier distance, robust reachable-set score, or learned surrogate) because local sensing and unknown target dynamics invalidate exact certificates.
5. Support recruitment becomes a marginal problem: add supporter `k` to target `j` only if `C_{S∪{k},j}` or its continuous margin crosses/improves a threshold enough to justify removing `k` from coverage.

This is materially stronger than nearest-target assignment or raw distance heuristics because the allocation edge already incorporates **speed, geometry, target objective, capture semantics, and possibly obstacles**.

[AI判断] P0028 supplies the most directly usable structure:

[AI判断] P0020 adds the critical obstacle correction: in clutter, an “angle to open space” is not enough. The relevant escape sector should ideally be weighted by **geodesic/intercept time or approximate reachability**, because walls/no-fly regions can help one side and hurt the other.

# Major assumption gaps relative to CoCap

1. **Information** — P0016/P0020/P0021 and the allocation papers rely on full or very strong state/model information; P0025 uses an explicit non-anticipative/Stackelberg convention. P0028 is distributed among pursuers but still gives each pursuer the evader position. CoCap’s local/intermittent enemy observability is much weaker.
2. **Dynamics** — much of the clean geometry assumes first-order simple motion and instantaneous heading changes. CoCap’s UAV action interface, acceleration/yaw limits, inertia, and collision dynamics can destroy straight-line/Apollonius/barrier formulas.
3. **Known opponent capability** — speed bounds and often target dynamics are known. CoCap may only estimate or learn them.
4. **Capture semantics** — point capture (P0020/P0021/P0025) vs positive-radius capture (P0024/P0028) materially changes geometry. The certificate must match CoCap’s exact success condition.
5. **Environment** — analytical allocation P0025 is convex/straight-boundary; P0021 is obstacle-free; P0028 is open-plane. P0020 shows obstacles can alter the strategic geometry itself.
6. **Centralized/global assignment information** — matching/ILP layers typically assume the whole feasibility graph is available. CoCap seeks decentralized/local execution and dynamic support recruitment.
7. **Static/fixed teams** — the theory rarely captures repeated target arrivals, target loss/re-detection, dynamic coalition switching, or agents returning to coverage after capture.
8. **Deterministic guarantees** — exact winning regions assume model correctness. CoCap’s sensing noise, learning errors, stochastic exploration, and function approximation require robust/probabilistic margins.
9. **Coupling/safety** — pairwise certificates can ignore interference between simultaneously assigned subgames, collision avoidance, communication congestion, and resource competition unless modeled explicitly.
