# READING NOTE — P0035 — TERL large-scale multi-target encirclement

## 1. Paper positioning
- Problem: [原文] Large-scale multi-robot / multi-target **encirclement** with static obstacles, collision risk and environmental disturbances.
- Main predecessors: [原文] Single-target pursuit/encirclement RL; large-scale collision-avoidance pursuit; distributional RL; Transformer relational reasoning.
- Why this paper was needed: [原文] Single-target policies do not naturally solve target selection or safe coordination when many pursuers and evaders coexist; concatenation/mean aggregation also degrades with variable entity counts.
- [AI判断] TERL is a direct novelty threat to any CoCap story built around “Transformer + multi-target encirclement + target selection + count generalization.” Its key limitation for CoCap is not network capacity but **information semantics**: all active target states are globally available.

## 2. Problem setting
- Agents / targets: [原文] \(N\) pursuers and \(M\) evaders in a bounded 2-D arena. Training curriculum reaches 15 pursuers / 4 evaders; evaluation reaches 80 / 20.
- Dynamics: [原文] Circular robots with identical kinematic constraints; arena includes static obstacles and Rankine-vortex disturbance fields.
- Action space: [原文] Discrete pair \(a_t=[a,\omega]\), linear acceleration plus angular velocity. Evaluation uses \(a\in\{-0.4,0,0.4\}\) m/s² and \(\omega\in\{-\pi/6,0,\pi/6\}\) rad/s; pursuer max speed 3.0 m/s.
- Sensing / observation: [原文] Teammates and obstacles are local within a fixed perceptual radius \(r_{percept}\); all **non-encircled evaders are observable regardless of distance**. The paper does not give a numerical value for \(r_{percept}\) in the main text; it remains a symbolic fixed radius.
- Communication: [原文] No peer-to-peer inter-agent communication is allowed. The authors explicitly describe one-way target broadcasting as a practical alternative; evader positions are therefore available to all pursuers.
- Centralized/decentralized: [原文] Global information is used to design training rewards; during execution each robot independently acts from its observation and a shared policy. This is CTDE in the broad sense, but the core method is IQN/value-based and should not be relabeled as a centralized-critic actor–critic method.
- Map / obstacles: [原文] Bounded 2-D space, soft boundary, random static obstacles, Rankine vortices.
- Target behavior: [原文] Evaders use an artificial-potential-field (APF) heuristic.
- Speed ratio: [原文] Evader max speed 3.5 m/s vs pursuer 3.0 m/s; evaders also have a denser angular-velocity action set.
- Capture / termination definition: [原文] An evader is encircled when at least three pursuers are within \(d_{encircle}\) and the angular gaps satisfy the prescribed uniformity constraints. Encircled evaders are immobilized and removed from active interaction; pursuers continue to remaining targets. Episode ends when all evaders are encircled, fewer than three pursuers remain active, or the time limit is reached.

## 3. Core idea
### Plain-language summary
[原文] TERL keeps each observed entity as a separate token, uses Transformer attention to model relations, then applies a target-selection attention stage over active evaders before an IQN action head. A global cooperation reward discourages too many pursuers from clustering on one target.

[AI判断] The “target selection” is **attention-based prioritization**, not a team-level hard assignment. Pursuer–target subgroups emerge from independent policies and reward shaping; no explicit assignment matrix or resource-feasibility constraint is enforced.

### Mathematical / algorithmic mechanism
#### Encirclement criterion
[原文] Eq. (1) requires, for each evader \(j\), at least three pursuers inside the encirclement radius and a bounded maximum angular gap:

\[
|\{p_i\mid d(p_i,p_j)\le d_{encircle}\}|\ge 3,
\qquad \max(\phi)\le \psi.
\]

[原文] In implementation, \(\psi=\pi\) and an additional balance condition is used:

\[
\max(\phi)\le \kappa\min(\phi),\qquad \kappa=3.
\]

[AI判断] This is a meaningful multi-agent geometric enclosure definition, stronger than one-agent proximity. It is still an **instantaneous success condition**, not a game-theoretic certificate that the current coalition can force future capture.

#### Reward
[原文] Safety term Eq. (3):

\[
R_{d1}=\begin{cases}
-80,&d_{min}<0\ \text{(collision)},\\
-5,&0\le d_{min}<d_{safe}.
\end{cases}
\]

Target-proximity guidance Eq. (4), for each evader:

\[
R_{d2}=\begin{cases}
5,&d_{safe}\le d_e\le d_{encircle},\\
5\exp[-0.05(d_e-d_{encircle})],&d_e>d_{encircle}.
\end{cases}
\]

and \(R_d=R_{d1}+\sum R_{d2}\).

[原文] Balanced-selection reward Eq. (5) counts \(P\), pursuers within \(d_{related}=3d_{encircle}\) of an evader:

\[
R_c=\begin{cases}
-10,&P\ge 5,\\
5\max(0,1-0.3(P-3)),&3\le P<5.
\end{cases}
\]

If one target has \(P<3\) while another has \(P>5\), all pursuers receive −10. Crossing the boundary gives −5.

[原文] Completion reward is proportional to angular uniformity, reported as

\[
R_{completion}=120\frac{2\pi}{n_k}e^{-\sigma},
\]

where \(n_k\) is the number of encircling pursuers and \(\sigma\) is the standard deviation of their encirclement angles; each timestep also incurs −1.

## 4. Method details
### RL/MARL papers
- Observation: [原文] \(o=[o_{ego},o_{team},o_{evader},o_{obstacle}]\), all in ego frame:
  - \(o_{ego}=[v_x,v_y,d_{nearest},pursuit\ status]\);
  - \(o_{team}=[p_x,p_y,v_x,v_y,d,\theta,pursuit\ status]\);
  - \(o_{evader}=[p_x,p_y,v_x,v_y,d,\theta,heading\ error]\);
  - \(o_{obstacle}=[p_x,p_y,r,d,\theta]\).
- Entity cardinality: [原文] Implementation caps detectable teammates at 5, obstacles at 5, evaders at 8 during training; entities are sorted by distance, zero-padded when absent, and masked. The evader cap can be changed at evaluation.
- Action: [原文] 3×3 discrete acceleration/angular-rate combinations.
- Reward: [原文] Eqs. (3)–(5), completion reward and time/boundary penalties described above.
- Architecture: [原文] Four stages: Observation Embedding → Relation Extraction → Target Selection → Action Output.
- Actor/Critic/Q: [原文] Value-based IQN estimates return distributions / action values; final action is argmax over expected action-value distribution.
- CTDE: [原文] Global information is permitted in training reward construction; execution removes global training-only information. However, globally broadcast active-evader states remain part of each execution observation by task design.
- Replay/on-policy: [原文] IQN/distributional value-learning framework.
- Curriculum/exploration: [原文] Curriculum increases problem size from 3P/1E to 15P/4E over 7 million steps.

### Transformer / target-selection pipeline
1. [原文] **Type-specific entity embeddings:** ego, teammate, obstacle and evader vectors are each embedded by dedicated MLPs into a common feature dimension.
2. [原文] **Type embeddings + Transformer relation extraction:** type ID \(t\in\{0,1,2,3\}\) is added; multi-head self-attention computes pairwise entity interactions. For ego query \(q_0\), Eq. (6):

\[
r_{0,j}=\operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{q_0k_j^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)v_j.
\]

3. [原文] **Pooling:** max-pooling over Transformer output \(M\) forms a compact global relational feature. This is concatenated with ego representation.
4. [原文] **Target selection:** the combined feature is the query; evader representations are keys/values. Attention forms a weighted target context, concatenated back to the query and passed to the action-value head.
5. [AI判断] The target attention weights are not binary assignment variables; there is no constraint that each pursuer selects exactly one target or that each target receives a required capacity.

### Capturability / assignment audit using R-PE-01
- Target selection: **YES**, adaptive attention/prioritization.
- Explicit team-level assignment: **NO**.
- Capacity constraint: **NO hard constraint**. Eq. (5) is a soft crowding/resource-balancing reward based on current nearby counts.
- Dynamic coalition recruitment: only **emergent**; agents can shift focus and continue to uncaptured targets, but subgroup membership is not represented/optimized as a coalition decision.
- **No coalition capturability certificate.** The geometric terminal criterion says when encirclement has happened; it does not certify that coalition \(S\) can force it from the current state.
- [AI判断] Relative to P0024/P0025, TERL learns a flexible preference/action policy but does not construct `guaranteed feasible edge → constrained assignment`.

## 5. Evidence
- Baselines: [原文] IQN, DQN, Mean Embedding. These remove Relation Extraction and Target Selection and use mean/average aggregation variants.
- Metrics: [原文] success rate, travel time, collision ratio.
- Ablations: [原文] remove Relation Extraction or remove Target Selection. Relation Extraction is critical for large/constrained scenarios; removing Target Selection mainly worsens completion efficiency and heavily reduces constrained-case success.
- Simulation: [原文] Training reaches 15P/4E; testing spans small, medium and large teams plus constrained coordination (CC).
- Real experiment: [原文] None.
- Scalability/generalization: [原文] The model trained up to 15 pursuers / 4 targets is evaluated **without retraining** on up to 80 pursuers / 20 targets, where the paper reports 100% success in standard large-scale settings. The mechanism is entity-wise relational encoding, shared policy, masking/padding and attention that does not rely on one fixed concatenated joint-state vector.
- [原文] CC case uses 28 pursuers / 14 evaders, only a 2:1 ratio despite the ≥3-pursuer terminal requirement; TERL substantially outperforms baselines, indicating dynamic reuse of pursuers after targets are completed.

## 6. Limitations
### Author-stated [原文]
- The paper focuses on simulation and identifies large-scale relational modeling as its main bottleneck; it does not claim realistic onboard perception or strategic opponent learning.

### Evidence-visible [原文 + AI判断]
- Active target positions/velocities are globally available regardless of distance.
- Peer-to-peer communication is removed, but replaced by exogenous one-way target broadcast.
- Evaders are APF heuristic, not learned/self-play opponents.
- No explicit resource-constrained assignment matrix or coalition feasibility check.
- `r_percept` is described but no numerical main-text value was found.
- Motion is 2-D with a discrete \((a,\omega)\) action set; no physical UAV deployment.
- After all targets are encircled, the episode ends; there is no return to coverage/search or repeated target arrivals.

### Our inference [AI判断]
- TERL and CoCap should **not** be differentiated primarily by Transformer architecture. The sharper distinction is information and task semantics: global target broadcast + capture-only multi-target progression versus genuinely local/intermittent target knowledge + persistent coverage/capture lifecycle.
- Transformer and generic target selection can no longer be primary CoCap contributions.

## 8. Relation to CoCap
- Closest similarities: same high-level multi-target encirclement objective; heterogeneous entity observations; Transformer relational aggregation; target prioritization; acceleration/angular-rate actions; obstacles; agents move on to remaining targets after one is encircled.
- Fundamental differences: target state is global/broadcast; no explicit assignment/capacity; no capturability-aware recruitment; APF target; no persistent coverage/search; no post-capture recovery to coverage; no repeated arrivals.
- Does it cover a candidate novelty? It **kills** novelty for Transformer-based multi-target encirclement, attention target selection, and large-team count generalization. It partially covers target-wise adaptive focus and post-target retasking. It does not cover strict local sensing + learned coalition feasibility + dynamic support recruitment + coverage recovery.
- Directly reusable ideas: variable-cardinality entity-token encoder, masking/padding, relation extraction, target-attention head, large-scale count-generalization evaluation protocol.
- Non-transferable assumptions: global active-target broadcast and heuristic evaders.
- New research questions exposed: can a target-selection head be upgraded from “preference” to an uncertainty-aware **capturability/resource decision** while preserving variable-cardinality scalability?
