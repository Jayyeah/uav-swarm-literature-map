# READING NOTE — P0033 — Role-based MADDPG for multi-target UAV pursuit/exploration

## 1. Paper positioning
- Problem: [原文] Multi-UAV / multi-target pursuit-evasion in cluttered 2-D space, with some agents tracking already identified targets and other agents exploring overlooked regions.
- Main predecessors: [原文] MADDPG/CTDE; prior swarm-monitoring work; classical pursuit/evasion and distributed pursuit; Voronoi exploration.
- Why this paper was needed: [原文] The authors argue that most learning-based multiplayer PE work remained single-target, while multi-target pursuit additionally requires balancing exploration for undiscovered targets against exploitation/tracking of known targets.
- [AI判断] Its real contribution is a **problem/lifecycle predecessor** for concurrent exploration + multi-target tracking, not an allocation/capturability paper.

## 2. Problem setting
- Agents / targets: [原文] Simulation uses multiple pursuers and multiple evaders plus exploratory scouts. Main double-target experiments use 5 pursuers vs 2 evaders; Role-based MADDPG adds 5 scouts. Real experiment uses 3 scouts + 2 pursuers + 1 evader.
- Dynamics: [原文] Core simulation is OpenAI Multi-Agent Particle Environment (MPE): continuous 2-D positions/velocities and continuous 2-D motion commands. This is not a full quadrotor model.
- Action space: [原文] Continuous 2-D motion command in MPE.
- Sensing / observation: [原文] Section IV states the MPE state-space vector (also used as observation vector) contains 2-D position and velocity. The paper assumes target location can be propagated through the communication network. It does **not** specify a literal finite FOV/range/occlusion observation model comparable to P0034.
- Communication: [原文] Section IV assumption (b): agents propagate target location to neighboring agents through a communication network. Exact range/topology/message timing are not formalized beyond neighbor propagation.
- Centralized/decentralized: [原文] MADDPG uses centralized critics with joint observations/actions in training and decentralized actors at execution. The authors call the role-based variant “semi-decentralized training” because agents receive role-specific rewards, while retaining centralized critics.
- Map / obstacles: [原文] 6 m × 6 m simulation with randomized static obstacles and randomized initial conditions.
- Target behavior: [原文] Evaders are learning competitors trained in the same competitive setting; they receive a collision/attack penalty and learn to evade.
- Speed ratio: [原文] Targets are set to be 30% faster than pursuers in simulation.
- Capture / termination definition: [原文] A successful **attack** is defined by pursuer–evader collision for reward purposes, but reported mission success is primarily **tracking/proximity**: average minimum pursuer-target distance below the 0.5 m sensory range. There is no N-agent enclosure/no-escape capture criterion and no post-capture immobilization/release lifecycle.

## 3. Core idea
### Plain-language summary
[原文] Rather than forcing every pursuer to chase known targets, the method defines two heterogeneous behavior classes: pursuers receive tracking/attack rewards, while scouts receive a Voronoi exploration reward. MADDPG then learns continuous policies for these role classes.

[AI判断] The important distinction is that this is **role-conditioned policy learning**, not an online role allocator. The multi-target experiment also manually splits the pursuit team into sub-teams for different targets rather than learning an explicit constrained assignment.

### Mathematical / algorithmic mechanism
[原文] MADDPG follows CTDE. For agent \(i\), actor update uses the deterministic policy gradient

\[
\nabla_{\theta_i}J \approx \frac{1}{K}\sum_j \nabla_{\theta_i}\mu_i(o_i^j)\nabla_{a_i}Q_i^{\mu}(O^j,A^j)\big|_{a_i=\mu_i(o_i^j)}.
\]

The role-specific rewards are the paper's main modification.

Boundary penalty, Eq. (1):

\[
B_i=-\min(\exp(3|x_i|-9),c_1)-\min(\exp(3|y_i|-9),c_2).
\]

Collision penalty, Eq. (2):

\[
C_i=\begin{cases}-c_3,&Dist(i,j)\le R(i)+R(j),\\0,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
\]

Pursuer reward, Eq. (3):

\[
R_{p_i}=B_i+C_i+CR_{p_i}+DR_{p_i},
\]

where \(CR_{p_i}\) is the positive target-contact reward and \(DR_{p_i}\) is distance shaping.

Evader reward, Eq. (4):

\[
R_{e_i}=B_i+C_i+CR_{e_i}.
\]

Scout reward, Eq. (5):

\[
R_{s_i}=B_i+C_i+E_i.
\]

[原文] Algorithm 1 constructs the Voronoi diagram and uses `Vor Reward -= Max(Voronoi Areas)`, pushing scouts to reduce the largest Voronoi cell and spread through space.

## 4. Method details
### RL/MARL papers
- Observation: [原文] MPE position/velocity state; target location propagation to neighbors is assumed. No explicit sensor dropout/FOV/occlusion state encoding is given.
- Action: [原文] Continuous MPE motion.
- Reward: [原文] Eqs. (1)–(5) above. Numerical simulation values include +20 for pursuer-target collision, −10 for evader being hit, and −10 collision-avoidance penalty.
- Architecture: [原文] Role-based extension of MADDPG; the heterogeneity is induced by role-specific reward functions.
- Actor/Critic/Q: [原文] Individual decentralized actors; centralized critics have access to joint observations/actions in training.
- CTDE: [原文] Yes.
- Replay/on-policy: [原文] Off-policy MADDPG with replay buffer.
- Curriculum/exploration: [原文] Exploration is a task-level behavior from scout rewards, not merely action-noise exploration.

### Role and target-allocation semantics
- [原文] The paper says it “assign[s] two types of roles” and uses separate scout and pursuer rewards with explicit counts \(n_s,n_p\).
- [原文] In the two-target MADDPG experiment, “the pursuit team is split into two sub-teams for tracking targets.”
- **SCREEN CORRECTION / refinement:** The SCREEN wording “preassigned roles” is operationally correct, but the paper itself sometimes calls them “autonomous heterogeneous role assignments.” Full READ finds no learned role-gating variable, switching policy, target-allocation optimization, or join/leave rule that would justify interpreting this as dynamic role assignment. It is best described as **predefined role classes whose behaviors are learned**.
- [AI判断] There is no explicit target assignment matrix, no target capacity constraint, no online subgroup formation, and no dynamic support recruitment.

### Capturability audit using R-PE-01
- [原文] The authors empirically increase the number of pursuers in a two-target scenario until average minimum distance falls below the 0.5 m sensory range; \(n_p=5\) is then chosen.
- [AI判断] This is empirical sizing, not a coalition winning test. It does not answer whether a particular coalition \(S\) can guarantee capture of target \(j\) from the current state.
- **No coalition capturability certificate.**
- [AI判断] Consequently, the paper does not instantiate the R-PE-01 chain `certificate → feasible edge/hyperedge → constrained assignment`.

## 5. Evidence
- Baselines: [原文] Multi-target MADDPG without scouts vs Role-based MADDPG with scouts.
- Metrics: [原文] Agent rewards; minimum/average/maximum target distance; approximate sensory coverage.
- Ablations: [原文] Number-of-pursuers and number-of-scouts sweeps are used to choose team composition; not a modern mechanism ablation.
- Simulation: [原文] Double-target case: 5 pursuers vs 2 faster targets; role-based case adds 5 scouts. Average minimum distance is about 0.46–0.47 m after training. With 5 scouts, average overall sensory coverage is about 35% of the 6 m × 6 m region.
- Real experiment: [原文] 6 Crazyflie 2.1 drones: 3 scouts, 2 pursuers, 1 evader; 3 traffic cones. An external computer performs computation; Crazyradio PA communicates commands; Loco Positioning Decks + 8 UWB anchors provide localization; Flowdeck provides height sensing. Average final pursuer-target distance is 0.4983 m, below the stated 0.5 m sensing range.
- Scalability/generalization: [原文] No evidence of variable-cardinality zero-shot policy scaling. Simulation multi-target evidence does not transfer directly to the physical test, which is single-target.

## 6. Limitations
### Author-stated [原文]
- Future work includes generalizing to application-specific behaviors and stronger partial observability / emergent communication.

### Evidence-visible [原文 + AI判断]
- Core simulation uses abstract MPE dynamics.
- “Decentralized” does not mean no centralized training or no target communication.
- Target locations are propagated through neighbor communication.
- Real hardware depends on external compute and UWB infrastructure.
- Multi-target subteams are split rather than learned through explicit assignment.
- Success is tracking/proximity oriented; no no-escape geometric capture.

### Our inference [AI判断]
- The paper is a strong threat to broad claims such as “first to combine exploration and multi-target UAV pursuit,” but a weak threat to dynamic coverage↔capture role switching, capturability-aware support recruitment, or post-capture recovery.

## 8. Relation to CoCap
- Closest similarities: concurrent exploration and pursuit roles; multi-UAV; multi-target simulation; faster evasive targets; obstacles; decentralized actor execution; physical UAV demonstration.
- Fundamental differences: fixed role classes; target-state propagation; manual subteam split; tracking rather than coalition capture; no return-to-coverage after capture; no repeated arrivals; abstract simulation dynamics.
- Does it cover a candidate novelty? It **kills** broad novelty for “exploration/scouting + multi-target pursuit.” It does **not** cover the full dynamic local-sensing→support-recruitment→capture→recovery lifecycle.
- Directly reusable ideas: separate coverage/exploration and pursuit reward channels; simultaneous scout/pursuer baseline; real-system sanity checks for external localization assumptions.
- Non-transferable assumptions: fixed scout/pursuer identities, target-location propagation, manual target subteams, MPE motion.
- New research questions exposed: whether CoCap can make role membership and support recruitment state-dependent rather than predefined, and whether nonparticipants can maintain coverage while a dynamically chosen coalition captures.
